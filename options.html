When tabs go to sleep

Put tabs to sleep after without use

A sleeping tab stays in your tab bar with its title and icon. The page is unloaded from memory and loads again when you click the tab.

Keep these awake

To spot unsaved typing, TabGeek needs to look at the page just before it goes to sleep. Your browser will ask for access to websites. TabGeek only checks the form boxes on the page and never sends anything anywhere.

Sites that never sleep

Tabs on these sites are never put to sleep automatically. A site also covers its subdomains, so google.com covers mail.google.com.
